What the platform does

🎯

Finds your real weak spots

Every problem you've solved is mapped onto a unified topic taxonomy, so "I'm bad at DP" becomes a measured per-topic solve rate you can act on.

🔀

Recommends across platforms

Difficulty is normalised onto one scale, so a Codeforces rating and a LeetCode Hard can be compared — and your AtCoder practice can be answered with the right Codeforces problem.

📈

Targets the right difficulty

Recommendations sit just above your current level — hard enough to teach you something, not so hard you stall.

🏷️

Fills in missing metadata

AtCoder publishes no topic tags, so CPRS predicts them with a classifier trained on tagged Codeforces statements — 9,000+ problems made searchable by topic.

📊

Reports your progress

A per-platform report of every topic you've touched, your strengths, your weak areas and whether your recent form is improving.

🏆

Tracks your contest form

Your full rating history on every platform, with the patterns behind it — whether you've plateaued, how consistent your rounds are, and how far you are from your peak.

💬

Explains every suggestion

No black box. Each recommendation states why it was picked — which weak topic it targets and how it matches your level.
